Hinglish – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Overview

Hinglish (“the App”) is an English-learning app for Hindi speakers. This policy explains what information the App handles. In short: the App works entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit or share any personal information.

2. Information we collect

  • No personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or location.
  • No internet usage. This version of the App does not request the internet permission and does not make network requests or connect to any server.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising. The App contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not use advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts. You do not need to register or sign in.

3. Children

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is no personal data of children to share, sell or disclose.

4. Educational content

All learning material (vocabulary, grammar, quizzes, flashcards and example sentences) is provided for general educational and self-study purposes on an “as is” basis. We take care to keep it accurate but do not guarantee it is error-free or suitable for any specific examination or official purpose.

5. Future changes (advertising)

We may introduce advertising (such as Google AdMob) in a future update. If we do, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will describe the data involved (for example the advertising ID, IP address and device information handled by the ad provider), how to opt out, and any consent request shown where required by law. Ads would require an internet connection.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, and the “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer at support.info247@gmail.com.
