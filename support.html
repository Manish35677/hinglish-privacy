Common questions

Do I need internet to use Hinglish?
No. The lessons, quizzes and flashcards work offline. Internet is only used to display the Privacy Policy page inside the app.

I found a mistake in a lesson or quiz.
Email us the screen name and the correction you suggest, and we will review it.

The app is not working properly.
Update to the latest version from Google Play, then restart the app. If the problem continues, contact us with the details above.
